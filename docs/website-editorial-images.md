# Website editorial image set

Created for the New Colour website with the built-in image generation tool.
These are illustrative editorial scenes, not photographs of the company's
staff, office, client work, or campaign results. Existing project videos and
source reports remain the evidence of actual work.

Final web assets: `app/public/assets/editorial/` (1440px and 720px WebP versions).
The exact built-in generation prompts are in `website-editorial-prompts.json`.
Each source is composed in a 3:2 landscape frame, with the central subject
kept inside the middle half for responsive crops. No lettering, logos,
watermarks, simulated campaign results, or added claims.

## Prompts and placements

Every prompt uses this direction:

> Photorealistic natural editorial photography for a contemporary digital
> studio website. An observed, believable working moment with tactile
> materials, soft daylight, restrained contrast, ordinary imperfections,
> and accurate perspective. Royal blue and white are the dominant designed
> objects, with a small tomato-red accent; retain natural material colours.
> Landscape 3:2 framing. Keep the important subject in the central half and
> leave breathing room at the edges for portrait and landscape crops.
> No words, lettering, logos, watermarks, floating interface graphics, neon
> lighting, synthetic glossy surfaces, excessive bokeh, or staged smiles.

1. **video-editing.webp** — Video Production service. Side view of a working
   video editor at a silver laptop and external monitor in a modest daylight
   edit suite. The editor is seen from behind, hands below the desk edge.
   The screen contains a softly defocused timeline of blue and red clips,
   not legible software text. Wired headphones, a small external drive,
   worn grey desk surface, practical cabling. 50mm lens, natural exposure.
2. **cgi-materials.webp** — CGI & Rendering service. Close documentary view
   of a 3D artist's material reference desk: brushed aluminium sample,
   white ceramic sphere, cobalt-blue glass vessel, matte red sample card,
   beside a silver computer. An out-of-focus neutral wireframe object on
   the background monitor. Realistic reflections, fine dust and surface
   texture; no person, 50mm lens, soft north-facing window light.
3. **motion-storyboards.webp** — Motion Graphics service and motion Insight.
   A sequence of six paper storyboard frames loosely arranged on a white
   studio table, showing the same simple blue circle moving through white
   architectural openings, with one red frame marker. A graphite pencil,
   clear ruler and a closed blue notebook sit nearby. Top-down photograph,
   physical drawings with slight paper curl and pencil marks, no letters.
4. **graphic-print-proofs.webp** — Graphic Design service. Close editorial
   still life of overlapping unbranded white print proofs, precise royal-
   blue geometric compositions, a small red swatch and metal binder clips
   on a grey worktable. Show genuine paper fibres, trimmed edges, slight
   misalignment and natural shadows. No printed text. 50mm lens, daylight.
5. **social-content.webp** — Social Media Management service. Candid
   overhead view of a content planning desk: a silver phone showing an
   unbranded product photograph, several printed portrait-format photo
   thumbnails, an open blank weekly grid notebook with small blue and red
   stickers, a compact camera partly visible. White and blue materials,
   no hands, text, logos or performance figures. Soft late-morning light.
6. **website-planning.webp** — Website Insight and Studio Clarity. An
   overhead photograph of physical website wireframes on white paper,
   simple blue block layouts, a silver laptop showing a softly defocused
   neutral webpage, red pencil and metal ruler. A few paper edges overlap
   naturally. No readable text or logos. Practical working desk, even
   window light, clear material detail, generous crop margins.
7. **search-research.webp** — Search Insight and SEO research section.
   An open reference book with indistinct, unreadable print, a silver
   laptop at an angle showing a softly defocused search field, several
   blue page tabs, a single red bookmark and a white notebook on a real
   library worktable. Quiet research setting, bookshelves softly present
   in the background, believable daylight, 50mm lens, no readable text.
8. **campaign-planning.webp** — Paid media strategy story, with the existing
   autoplay hero and source reports preserved. Overhead
   photograph of a real planning table with three blank blue and white
   campaign layout sheets, paper audience grouping cards, red pencil,
   calculator and the corner of a silver laptop. The composition suggests
   comparing creative and budget choices without showing numbers, charts,
   platform logos or results. Lightly used materials, natural daylight.
9. **product-photography.webp** — Studio Character and supporting service
   imagery. Documentary photograph of a compact tabletop product shoot:
   an unlabelled cobalt-blue glass bottle on white paper sweep, a small
   red card used as a reflector, tripod-mounted camera and fabric softbox
   at the frame edge. Light stands and ordinary cabling, realistic glass
   reflection, no logos, no elaborate set, soft studio illumination.
10. **connected-workflow.webp** — Studio Continuity and supporting service
    imagery. Photograph of a physical project workflow board on a white
    table, four groups of blank blue cards linked by thin grey pencil
    lines, two small red approval stickers, silver laptop edge and a
    simple white notebook. Deliberately modest, tactile and useful;
    no readable text, floating graphics, numbers or digital overlays.
    Slightly oblique overhead viewpoint, natural daylight.
